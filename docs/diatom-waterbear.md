# WE / Diatom × Waterbear / Mnemonic Kernel v1

## Interpretation
The work's 40 records can be read through three conceptual surfaces: **diatom shell** (structured geometric boundary), **tardigrade/waterbear** (a living system able, in some species and conditions, to enter cryptobiotic states under stress), and **human thought** (the semantic/somatic storytelling medium). The motif is a creative interpretation, not a claim of literal microorganisms in the narrative or confirmed authorial intent.

## Five-step mnemonic
SEE: read a chapter scene and its observed event.
DO: select the associated shell / living-state / human-medium transformation.
LEARN: identify the conflict of agency and order.
RETAIN: preserve a compact phrase together with the relevant chapter ID.
WITNESS: keep source-derived observations distinct from metaphor and speculation.

## Eight mnemonic gates
- Gate 01, W001–W005: **SHELL FORMATION** — Construct identity, number, rigid geometry and the State's ordered glass envelope. The State is encoded as an engineered silica shell; D-503 begins inside its apparent security.
- Gate 02, W006–W010: **FIRST PERMEABILITY** — Unknown variables, unexpected desire and the first passage through an orderly boundary. Perforations in the boundary become a mnemonic for information and experience entering.
- Gate 03, W011–W015: **INTERNAL VARIATION** — The system senses contradictions: intimacy, uncertainty, hidden corridors and nonlinear response. The human medium records the conflict between fixed form and living response.
- Gate 04, W016–W020: **MEMORY UNDER STRAIN** — The narrator's changing identity makes continuity, recollection and evidence separable. Stress first exposes why retaining a stable external label does not preserve an inner state.
- Gate 05, W021–W025: **FRACTURE AND PASSAGE** — Boundary crossings and the competing worlds weaken the shell's authority. The shell's geometry remains visible while its exclusivity fails.
- Gate 06, W026–W030: **COMPRESSION AND CHOICE** — Attachment, threatened offspring, compassion and the apparent impossibility of final revolution. A survival mnemonic: retreat, choose, store and preserve possibilities without claiming a guaranteed rescue.
- Gate 07, W031–W035: **EXTREME STRESS** — Compulsory treatment, threatened seizure, collapsing routine and near-violence. The living system faces coercion and environmental collapse; its next state is not predetermined.
- Gate 08, W036–W040: **AFTER THE OPERATION** — Memory gaps, breached walls, fractured trust and the engineered cessation of desire. W040 is a state-transition warning: persistent handwritten form does not authenticate continuity of feeling.

## Three examples
- W001: an ordered glass city = structured frustule mnemonic; living identity inside is not exhausted by the geometry.
- W037: birds appear within the city after the Green Wall is reportedly breached = shell boundary permeability, not literally a silica frustule cracking.
- W040: a handwriting pattern persists while D-503 reports altered feelings = external record versus inner experiential continuity. This is not tardigrade cryptobiosis; it demonstrates why a surviving form does not authenticate a surviving mental state.
