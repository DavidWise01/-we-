## Operating invariant
One governing objective, one committed action per decision, one-way evidence progression.
'Hegemonic' means a single ranked objective and one authoritative decision output, NOT unlimited power.
'Diodic' means forward-only append-only receipts; no rewriting earlier evidence or retroactive relabeling.
'No choice' means the controller emits exactly one best permitted action without human selection menus. Abstention is an actual action when all interventions fail the safety gate.
Three gates, in order: survival constraints; conservation/budget; maximum estimated net long-term value.
Tie-breaking deterministic: (1) survival margin, (2) net value, (3) lower energy cost, (4) abstain over unnecessary action, (5) stable action string order.
All threshold changes trained/declared before held-out evaluation.
Use one common synthetic source genealogy honestly; no independent-physical validation claims.
