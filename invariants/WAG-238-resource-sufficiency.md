# WAG-238 · Resource Sufficiency and Recovery Feasibility

.dlw · {24/[2]/[4]/(3)/(3)/(1)/(1)}

WAG-238 tries to distinguish sustainable resources, temporary shortages, and persistent deficits. It reconstructs past generation from observed changes in energy plus the realized cost of defense, switching and repair reported by the instrumented actuator after each completed tick; samples at energy saturation are censored. It compares short and long trailing receipts against an approximate minimal action cost, classifying status UNKNOWN, SUFFICIENT, TEMPORARY or INSUFFICIENT. BOOST is rejected during inferred persistent insufficiency, and during temporary shortage when current energy is below 0.55. It never reads scenario identity to choose actions or future forcing; the training table remains the frozen WAG-235 table. The controller receives a scalar measured outlay after the tick; it never receives the environment label. Instrumentation is an assumption that may not generalize to real sensors.

These classes are **heuristic inferences**, not mathematical proofs of actual indefinite recoverability. A negative past budget does not guarantee persistent future insufficiency, and a positive recent budget does not guarantee future safety. Censoring creates uncertainty and can suppress classification. The 'insufficient' class must not be represented as ground truth.
