# WAG-197 — Intermittent persistence
Executed Python estimator experiment, 480 paired sequences, 414720 checked updates, seeds 197–228. Baseline commitment after 96 samples of signed 0.09±0.02 pp; four cycles of exact-zero gaps and 96 renewed samples, or 512 quiet/opposing samples. Hold timers 1,8,32. All preserve uninterrupted commitment through 32 and 128 sample gaps; all release and recommit in every 256 sample gap. Longer hold adds release latency without improving these tested gap outcomes. No claim of exact amplitude reversal. Designed thresholds and gap lengths, no noisy interruptions tested. Next: distinguish known observation silence from measured zero and missing data. .dlw
