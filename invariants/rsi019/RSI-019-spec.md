# RSI-019 — Persistent delivery
RSI-018 extended with an atomic outbox: every new record creates three delivery obligations in the same SQLite transaction as archive, index and signed checkpoint. Endpoint Ed25519 receipts bind stream, index, replica identity, leaf digest and originating checkpoint. Readback precedes signing; retries after lost acknowledgments are idempotent. 80 local tests pass (12 new, 68 inherited). Synthetic keys and local replicas do not establish independent remote storage. A receipt establishes a historical assertion, not current availability. Audit checks receipt signatures but does not prove completeness of the outbox or validate every unsigned packet against the journal. Existing records require explicit backfill, not implemented. No physical power loss or network test. Next: fresh replica challenges and reconciliation after post-acknowledgment loss.
