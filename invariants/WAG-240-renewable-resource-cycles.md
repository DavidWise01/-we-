A finite external store of capacity 0.45 starts at 0.20 energy units. Renewable input is explicit exogenous supply 0.0018 + 0.0075 * max(0,sin(2π t / 96 + phase)) per simulated tick. This supply is generated by an outside source; it is not free energy or an emergent property of the controller. Excess harvested energy spills at storage capacity. Transfers remove no more than 0.012/tick from storage and arrive after 3 ticks with 16% loss and a 0.001-unit activation fee. Any in-transit withdrawals remaining at the final tick are accounted for. Background generation and pressure dynamics follow WAG-239, but forcing is extended by independently seeded 240-tick segments, so this is not one continuous high-fidelity physical forcing trace.
